This privacy policy applies to the KRG Power - بژمێری کارەبا mobile app (the “App”), published on Google Play and the Apple App Store by Bzhar Badawee, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). It explains what information the App handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. The App is an electricity bill calculator for the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, with an optional community map for reporting electricity problems.

Short version: the App does not require an account and never asks for your name unless you choose to send a solar inquiry. Your appliances and bills stay on your phone. Things you deliberately post to the community map — reports, photos, comments and likes — are public. The App shows ads from Google AdMob.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the App's local storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or anyone else:

  • the appliances you add (name, wattage, hours of use, days per month, quantity);
  • saved bills and usage history;
  • your settings, such as language, billing cycle and notification preferences;
  • a random identifier generated by the App on first launch (see section 2.2).

Bill calculations are performed entirely on your device using the published Kurdistan Regional Government tariff. Settings → Reset All Data removes your appliances and settings. Uninstalling the App removes everything above, including saved bills and the random identifier.

2. Information you choose to send

Some features send information to our backend, which is hosted on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). Nothing in this section is sent unless you actively use the feature.

2.1 Community reports — public

When you report an electricity problem, the App sends:

  • the problem category and the description you write;
  • the location you place on the map. The App does not read your phone's GPS — the location is only the point you choose;
  • a photo, only if you choose to attach one from your photo library;
  • the time of the report.

Reports are published immediately and are visible to everyone who uses the App. Do not include personal information, such as names, phone numbers or faces, in your description or photo.

2.2 Likes and comments — public

You can like and comment on reports without creating an account. To count one like per device, the App uses the random identifier described in section 1. It is generated by the App, contains no personal information, and is not linked to your name, phone number or advertising ID.

  • Likes store that identifier and the time. Only the total number of likes is shown publicly.
  • Comments store the text you write, that identifier and the time. Comments are visible to everyone.

2.3 Solar inquiries — private

If you fill in the optional solar questionnaire, the App sends the details you enter, which may include your name, phone number and city, property type, your goal, roof space, bill range and electricity phase, together with your estimated consumption and a suggested system size. We use this only to contact you about a solar system, using the method you chose. Solar inquiries are not public and cannot be read by other users.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information from your device, including the advertising ID, IP address and general device information. The App requests non-personalized ads, so ads are not chosen based on your interests or browsing history. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. How we use information

  • to run the community map and show reports, likes and comments;
  • to respond to solar inquiries you send;
  • to keep the service working, remove abuse and fix problems;
  • to show ads, which fund the App.

We do not sell your information.

6. Who receives information

  • Everyone using the App can see community reports, photos, comments and like counts.
  • Google acts as our service provider: Firebase stores App data, AdMob serves ads, and Google Play / App Store provide crash and performance reports. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose information.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Reports, photos and comments stay until they are removed. We may remove content that is abusive, false or contains personal information.
  • Solar inquiries are kept only as long as needed to follow up with you.
  • Local data: Settings → Reset All Data removes appliances and settings; uninstalling the App removes all of it.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot always tell which report or comment is yours. To ask us to delete a report, photo, comment or solar inquiry, email bzharnew@gmail.com with enough detail to identify it (for example the date, the category and where on the map it was posted). We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Data sent by the App travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is stored on Google Firebase. Database rules restrict what the App can write and prevent solar inquiries from being read by anyone through the App. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't post anything sensitive.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Not affiliated with the government

The App is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Kurdistan Regional Government or its Ministry of Electricity. Tariff rates are taken from the official Runaki programme published at runaki.gov.krd. Results are estimates; your official bill is the one issued by your electricity provider.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
